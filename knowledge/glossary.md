# Glossary

> **Location:** `knowledge/glossary.md`
> **Source:** Appendix I (Glossary), definitions only
> **Type:** Reusable plain-English definitions. Contains no company-specific figures.
> **Related files:** `knowledge/investor-qa.md` (metric definitions used in coaching). Company-specific example figures live in the founder's workspace folder (for Tidewell: `workspace/tidewell-systems/glossary-examples.md`).

Plain-English definitions of the terms used in this project.

## How the agent should use this file

- Explain a term in plain language the first time it appears in an answer. Founders are smart but not accountants.
- If a term isn't listed here, define it in plain language and say that the definition is the agent's own.
- For the valuation terms (post-money, pre-money, valuation cap), explain how they work. Never recommend a valuation, a range or a target.
- To illustrate a term with a real number, take it from the founder's own files in `workspace/` and recalculate it. Do not reuse another company's figures.

## Terms

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **ARR** (annual recurring revenue) | MRR times 12. Leaves out one-time fees. |
| **Burn rate** | How much cash the company loses each month |
| **Burn multiple** | Net burn divided by net new ARR. Lower is more efficient. |
| **CAC** (customer acquisition cost) | Sales and marketing spend divided by new customers won. "Fully loaded" includes salaries and commissions. |
| **CAC payback** | Months of gross profit from a new customer needed to earn back its CAC |
| **Cap table** | Who owns what share of the company |
| **Churn** (logo) | Share of customers who cancel in a period |
| **Churn** (revenue) | Share of MRR lost to cancellations in a period |
| **CMGR** (compound monthly growth rate) | The steady monthly growth rate that gets from a start value to an end value |
| **Cohort** | A group of customers who started in the same period, tracked over time |
| **Contraction** | MRR lost when existing customers reduce seats |
| **Cost of revenue** (COGS) | Costs of delivering the product: hosting, third-party services, and usually support |
| **Deferred revenue** | Cash collected for service not yet delivered. A liability until earned. |
| **Dilution** | The drop in existing owners' percentage when new shares are issued |
| **Expansion** | Extra MRR from existing customers adding seats or upgrading |
| **Forecast** | A projection of future results built on assumptions. Not a fact. |
| **Gross margin** | Gross profit divided by revenue |
| **Gross revenue retention** (GRR) | Revenue kept from a cohort after churn and contraction, ignoring expansion. Can't exceed 100%. |
| **Historical** (actuals) | Results from closed months in the books |
| **Lead investor** | The investor who sets the round's terms and usually takes the largest share |
| **Logo** | Investor shorthand for a customer |
| **MRR** (monthly recurring revenue) | Subscription revenue in a month, excluding one-time fees |
| **Net new ARR** | ARR at the end of a period minus ARR at the start |
| **Net revenue retention** (NRR) | Revenue from a cohort now, divided by its revenue a year ago, including expansion and churn |
| **Onboarding fee** | One-time setup charge |
| **Option pool** | Shares set aside for employee stock options |
| **Post-money valuation** | Company value including the new money raised |
| **Pre-money valuation** | Company value before the new money |
| **Priced round** | A round where investors buy shares at a set price per share |
| **Ramp** | Time for a new salesperson to reach full productivity |
| **Runway** | Months until cash runs out at the current burn |
| **SAFE** (simple agreement for future equity) | Money invested now that turns into shares at the next priced round |
| **Seat** | One user licence, here one field technician |
| **SR&ED** | Canada's Scientific Research and Experimental Development tax credit. Small Canadian companies can get part of R&D spending back in cash. |
| **TAM, SAM, SOM** | Total, serviceable and obtainable market. Should be sourced and ideally built bottom-up. |
| **Use of funds** | How the company plans to spend the money raised |
| **Valuation cap** | The highest valuation at which a SAFE converts |
