# The Founders' 18-Month Forecast: Tidewell Systems

> **Source:** Appendix C (The founders' 18-month forecast)
> **Type:** Case facts (company-specific, not general reference knowledge)
> **Status:** **Forecast, not actual.** Nothing in this file has been verified.
> **Related files:** `01-overview.md` (Appendix A), `02-financial-history.md` (Appendix B)

The founders' plan runs from October 2026 to March 2028. It takes MRR from $107K to $426K, headcount from 14 to 46, and ends with $1.59M of cash after the $4M raise.

Everything in this appendix is forecast, not actual. Craig built it in a spreadsheet over two weekends.

**Conventions:** Quarterly tables are in $000s. Brackets are negative.

## MRR chart: actual vs. founders' forecast

*Chart title: "MRR: $107K today, $426K in the founders' plan by March 2028." Monthly recurring revenue, CAD. Source files: `04_mrr_bridge_monthly_actuals.csv` and `08_forecast_18_month_founders_plan.csv`.*

Text description of the chart (the image is not embedded in this file):

- **Actual (grey line):** MRR rises gradually from about $15K in October 2024 to $107K in September 2026.
- **Forecast (blue dashed line):** MRR rises from $107K to $426K in March 2028.
- The forecast line is steeper than the actual line from the start. There are visible upward steps around the turn of the year into early 2027, then a steady climb to the end of the plan.

## The founders' assumptions

The reasoning column is the founders' own explanation. **It has not been checked.**

| Assumption | Founders' value | Founders' reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| New customers per account executive per month, once ramped | 5 | "Priya closes 4 to 5 a month. New AEs will have a head of sales and better tools." |
| Months for a new AE to reach full productivity | 1 | "Our product is easy to demo." |
| Inbound new customers | 4 a month, growing 3% a month | "Our content is starting to compound." |
| Seats per new customer | 10 | "That's our average." |
| Monthly logo churn | 1.0% | "Most of our churn came from early customers on the old product." |
| Net seat expansion in the existing base | 1.0% a month | "Customers add technicians as they grow." |
| List price for new customers | $45, rising to $54 in January 2027 | "We are cheaper than the competitors we lose to." |
| Price increase for existing customers | 20% in January 2027, for every customer including legacy $39 and annual plans. No extra churn assumed. | "Nobody left when we raised prices for new customers in 2025." |
| Northern Comfort Group renewal | Renews March 2027 at 600 seats and $40, plus a $20,000 onboarding fee | "Their COO told us they want every location on Tidewell." Nothing is signed. |
| Cost of revenue | 12% of revenue | "A cushion over today's 8%." |
| Sales commissions | 10% of first-year contract value | Same as today |
| Average onboarding fee per new customer | $900 | Blend of small and large customers |
| Marketing programs | $32,000 in October 2026, growing 8% a month | "Pour fuel on what works." |
| Founder pay | $14,000 a month each, loaded, from January 2027 | "Market rate after the raise." |
| Office, audit and tools | G&A other costs rise from $12,800 to $28,800 a month from January 2027 | New office lease and a first audit |
| Seed round | $4,000,000 received January 2027 | Target close date |
| SR&ED refund | $240,000 received July 2027 | "R&D spend is up, so the claim will be bigger." |
| Cash | Net income plus the raise minus equipment | Receivables, payables and deferred revenue are not modelled |
