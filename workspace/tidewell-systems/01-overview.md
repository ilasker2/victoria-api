## Summary

Tidewell Systems Inc. is a Toronto software company with $1.29M in annual recurring revenue, 211 customers and 14 staff. It is raising a $4M seed round to close in January 2027 and has about 8 months of cash left.

## Founders and origin story

The founders, Jennifer (CEO) and Craig (CTO), met as software engineers at a large Canadian software company.

Craig's brother runs a 12-technician HVAC business in Mississauga and was booking jobs from a whiteboard and a group chat. They built him a scheduling tool on weekends, two other contractors asked for it, and they incorporated Tidewell in January 2024.

- Both are strong engineers.
- Neither has raised a priced round, built a financial model before this one, or managed a P&L.
- Jennifer handles sales and fundraising.
- Craig runs product and engineering.

## Product and customers

**Tidewell Dispatch** is scheduling and dispatch software for trades businesses with 3 to 30 field technicians: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pest control and appliance repair. It covers job booking, technician routing, quotes, invoices and automatic customer text messages ("your technician is 20 minutes away").

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Pricing | $45 per technician seat per month (list price since July 2025). 59 early customers still pay a legacy $39. |
| Billing | Monthly by card or invoice. 45 customers prepay annually. Annual plans can only be cancelled at renewal. |
| Onboarding fee | $1,200 one time for customers with 10 or more seats. Larger accounts are quoted. |
| Active customers (Sep 30, 2026) | 211, using 2,493 seats |
| Largest customer | Northern Comfort Group, a 31-location HVAC franchise network. 260 seats at a discounted $40, prepaid annually. Signed March 2026, renews March 2027. |
| How customers find Tidewell | Two account executives (outbound sales), inbound from content and search, partner referrals from a plumbing and HVAC supply distributor, and the founders' network |
| Competitors the founders name | Jobber and Housecall Pro for small contractors, ServiceTitan for larger ones, and spreadsheets or paper |

## Team (Sep 30, 2026)

14 people:

Bookkeeping is outsourced to a part-time contractor. There is no finance person.
