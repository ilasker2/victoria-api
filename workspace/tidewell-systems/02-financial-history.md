## How the books are kept

These are the bookkeeper's policies. They change how some numbers read.

- **Cost of revenue** includes hosting, SMS and telephony, and card processing fees. Customer success salaries are recorded in operating expenses, not cost of revenue.
- **SR&ED credits** are recorded as other income in the month the cash arrives, not in the year the R&D work was done.
- **Annual plans** are billed up front and recorded as deferred revenue, then recognized monthly. Annual plans can only be cancelled at renewal. Northern Comfort Group's $124,800 prepayment in March 2026 is the largest item, and $52,000 of it is still unearned at September 30, 2026.
- **Onboarding fees** are recognized in the month the customer signs.
- **Salaries** are loaded costs: pay plus payroll taxes and benefits.
- **No pipeline data.** The company does not track its sales pipeline in a CRM yet. There is no record of deals in progress.

## Data files

Customer names are anonymised except Northern Comfort Group. The files reconcile: seats times price per seat equals the MRR bridge, and MRR plus onboarding fees equals revenue on the income statement.
